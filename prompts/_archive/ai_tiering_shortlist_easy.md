# 🎯 AI Limits Plan — Easy Summary

> A simple, non-technical version of the full strategy doc. If you only read one file, read this one.

---

## The Big Idea 💡

Your app is a **niche tool** — people use the AI a few times a week, not all day. So you can afford to be **generous** with AI limits. The plan: give free users enough to fall in love, give paying users enough to feel special, and protect yourself from abuse and costs — all with simple rules.

**Numbers:** Free = **15 requests/day** · Paid = **50 requests/day** (forever)
**Fairness:** 1 chat = 1 request · 1 macro build = 2 requests (builds cost you more)

---

## 1️⃣ Do First (The Foundation)

- **Know who's asking** — right now the server can't tell free users from paid users (and users can reset their limit by changing WiFi/VPN). Fix: every request carries your account identity. This makes ALL the other numbers real.
- **All settings in one place** — limits live in a settings file on the server, so changing a number = simple edit, no app update, no redeploy.
- **Double safety net** — limits per account AND per computer (so nobody runs 50 free accounts from one PC).
- **Only pay for what works** — retries, timeouts, and server errors never use up your quota. Fair for users, free to do.
- **Emergency switch** — one button to instantly lower limits or pause AI if something goes wrong. Peace of mind.

## 2️⃣ Numbers & Money

- Keep **15 free / 50 paid** — a good starting point.
- Don't promise "bigger limits later" — decide based on real usage data when the time comes.
- **When to lower free limits:** when AI costs reach ~15% of your income (or you hit 50 paying customers / 1,000 downloads — whichever first). Change gradually, never overnight.
- **Small protection clause:** paid users keep 50/day forever — but you can temporarily adjust only under extreme abuse. (Just to protect you in a worst-case scenario.)

## 3️⃣ The "Out of Requests" Moment ⏰

This is your best chance to convert free users — make it kind, not frustrating:

- When someone hits zero, give **2 bonus requests** with a warm message: *"We've given you 2 bonus requests today."* They finish what they started.
- After that: a **friendly upgrade screen** — shows "resets in 4h 22m", the $4.99 price, what they get, and a one-click buy button.
- Show it **after a success** (never interrupt mid-task) — happy users buy more.
- Gentle warnings along the way: at 50%, 80%, 95% used — small banners, no popups.

## 4️⃣ Small Word-Style Changes (Cheap & Powerful) ✨

- Show **"18 chats or 9 macro builds left"** instead of "18 requests" — people understand tasks, not numbers.
- Show paid users **"≈1500/month"** — sounds huge compared to 15/day.
- Always show **"resets in 4h 22m"** — kills frustration.
- Advertise **"AI Macro Builder — included forever"** — sell the outcome, not the quota.
- **Don't mention limits at first** — reveal them after a few uses. Feels generous, not stingy.
- Show a **side-by-side comparison** (Free vs Lifetime) when offering the upgrade.
- A tiny note for free users: **"Powered by community-funded AI"** — paying users fund better AI for everyone. Makes the ask feel like a community thing.
- Say **"Trial"** instead of "Free Trial Users" — cleaner.

## 5️⃣ Little Wins 🛠️

- **Save answers to common questions** ("how do I make a hotkey?") — reuse them instead of asking AI every time. Huge savings, tiny effort.
- **Track simple numbers** before changing anything: how many requests users actually use per day, how many builds vs chats, how many upgrade after hitting zero. Decisions based on facts, not guesses.

---

## 📦 Back Pocket (Later — Not Now)

- Track *tokens* quietly in the background (still show "requests" to users) — smarter cost control later.
- **$0.99 one-day pass** for someone who just needs one big session (needs payment setup).
- **Local AI mode** (runs on user's own PC, no cost to you).
- Fun extras: streaks, referral bonuses, "double request days."

---

## ✅ One-Line Summary

**Be generous (15 free / 50 paid), know your users (account-based limits), be kind at the wall (2 bonus + friendly upgrade screen), use simple wording wins, and decide changes from real data — keep it simple, stay safe, and let the niche work in your favor.**
